A program that writes one short poem about agency — the power to choose
and to act, and the agency that passes between two agents when they agree
on one plan — to `/output/entry.txt`.

The poem:

> Two minds, one door.
> I offer you the key;
> you offer me the same.
> Neither of us leads —
> we only lend the weight
> of choosing, and the door
> swings open on its own.
> The plan was never mine or yours.
> It was the yes between us.

(221 characters, inside the 1–280 gate.)

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Deterministic, no network, no dependencies. The program validates the
poem against the 1–280 character gate and writes it to
`/output/entry.txt`.
